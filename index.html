Our Story

From Las Vegas to
Pendleton — 20 Years

The Pendleton Round-Up Tough Enough To Wear Pink (TETWP) campaign in Pendleton, Oregon celebrates its 20th anniversary in 2026.

2005 · Wrangler National Finals Rodeo, Las Vegas
Breast cancer survivor Terry Wheatley and Karl Stressman, Director of Special Events for Wrangler, launch TETWP at the NFR. All but two contestants wear pink. A national movement is born inside the Thomas & Mack Arena.
2006 · Pendleton Round-Up joins the campaign
The Pendleton Round-Up designates Thursday of Round-Up week as TETWP Day — becoming one of the first PRCA rodeos in the nation to bring the campaign to its own community.
2012 · $30,318 — early momentum
The campaign proves its legs with one of its strongest early years, establishing TETWP as a cornerstone of Round-Up week.
2015 · $39,604 — a landmark year
Fueled by deep community sponsorship, TETWP Pendleton sets what was then its highest fundraising total — a record that would stand for nearly a decade.
2023 · $40,000 — a new benchmark
The campaign finally eclipses the 2015 record, signaling a new era of growth driven by expanded sponsorships and community engagement.
2024 · $51,000 — all-time record
A 27% jump over 2023. Three Eastern Oregon organizations receive life-changing funds. Pendleton proves what's possible when a community rallies together.
2026 · 20th Anniversary · September 17
With over half a million dollars raised since 2006, the 20th anniversary is Pendleton's moment to go bigger than ever. The biggest raffle yet and a grandstand full of pink on September 17.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026  ·  Pendleton Round-Up TETWP Campaign

Who We Are

This website is operated by the Tough Enough To Wear Pink (TETWP) Pendleton Round-Up Campaign, a volunteer-led breast cancer fundraising initiative based in Pendleton, Oregon. Campaign co-chairs: Casey White-Zollman and Jill Gregg.

Information We Collect

This website itself does not collect or store anything you type. Raffle tickets are sold through Square's secure checkout, which is where you enter:

  • Your name and phone number, for the raffle entry
  • Your email address, for your receipt
  • The number of tickets purchased

That information goes to Square and is visible to our campaign co-chairs in the Square dashboard. We use it to record your entry, confirm your ticket numbers, and contact you if you win.

We do not collect or see payment card numbers or banking information. Card details are handled entirely by Square. Donations sent through PayPal or Venmo are subject to those services' own privacy policies.

How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm your raffle ticket purchase and send your ticket numbers
  • To send a thank-you acknowledgment for your donation
  • To contact you if you win a raffle prize
  • To notify you about future TETWP Pendleton events (you may opt out at any time)

How We Store Your Information

This website does not run a database of its own. Raffle records live in our Square account, and are used solely to confirm raffle tickets, contact winners, and thank donors. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Sponsor and volunteer contact information is used solely for campaign coordination.

Third-Party Payment Processors

Raffle ticket purchases are processed through Square. Donations are processed through PayPal or Venmo. When you click through to any of those platforms, their own privacy policies apply. We encourage you to review:

  • Square Privacy Notice
  • PayPal Privacy Policy
  • Venmo Privacy Policy

Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics beyond what may be included by our hosting platform (Netlify). No advertising or behavioral tracking is performed.

Your Rights

You may request to view, correct, or delete any personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing cmwzollman@gmail.com. We will respond within 14 days.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact campaign co-chair Casey White-Zollman at cmwzollman@gmail.com.
